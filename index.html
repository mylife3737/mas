Services & Matrimonial (Nikah) Portal

Islamic Marriage (Nikah) Guidelines

Masjid Nur offers consultation and officiating services for Islamic marriage ceremonies in strict compliance with Islamic Sharia and Florida state laws.

1
Civil Marriage License Valid civil license from Charlotte, Lee, or Sarasota County clerk.
2
Bride's Guardian (Wali) & Witnesses Presence of the Wali and two upright male Muslim witnesses.
3
Agreed Mahr (Dowry) Mutual agreement upon the bridal gift as commanded in Islam.
